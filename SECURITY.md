# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you find a significant vulnerability, or evidence of one, report it privately.

Use [GitHub's mechanism for privately reporting a vulnerability](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing/privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability#privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability). Open the "Security" tab of the affected repository. Click "Report a vulnerability" to open the advisory form.

A member of this organization triages your report. Triage means that the member examines the report and sets its priority. If we accept the vulnerability, we publish a security advisory. All further communication then happens in that security advisory.
